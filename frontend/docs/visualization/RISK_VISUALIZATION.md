==================================================
1. THE COLOR OF RISK
==================================================
- **Stability**: Calm Greens/Golds.
- **Uncertainty**: Muted Cyans.
- **High Risk**: Deep Crimson (#8B0000).

==================================================
2. PROBABILITY CLOUDS (D3.js)
==================================================
- Instead of single lines, use gradient-filled paths to show the 95% confidence interval. 
- The further the future, the wider the "Fan" of uncertainty becomes.

==================================================
3. INSTABILITY SHADERS (Three.js)
==================================================
- When a simulation enters a high-volatility period, the Bubble's surface becomes irregular and "jitters."
- This provides an immediate, subconscious understanding of financial instability.

==================================================
4. AI ANNOTATIONS
==================================================
- AI-identified risks are marked with a small red "marker" on the timeline and graph.
