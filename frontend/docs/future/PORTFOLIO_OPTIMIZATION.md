==================================================
1. OBJECTIVE
==================================================
Enable users to simulate entire portfolios of assets, calculating Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT) metrics visually.

==================================================
2. KEY FEATURES
==================================================
- **The "Galaxy" Visualization**: Moving from a single Bubble to a cluster of bubbles representing different assets.
- **Efficient Frontier Mapping**: Visualizing the trade-off between risk and return.
- **Rebalancing Simulations**: Visualizing the impact of monthly/annual rebalancing on long-term wealth.

==================================================
3. TECHNICAL CHALLENGES
==================================================
- Increased computational complexity for Monte Carlo engines (Matrix operations).
- AI analysis of multi-asset correlations.
