1. THE CURIOUS INVESTOR (Retail)
==================================================

- **Value**: The immersive Bubble system makes their future feel "real."

2. THE FINANCE STUDENT
==================================================
- **Goal**: Build intuition for academic models (DCF, WACC).
- **Pain**: Textbook formulas are abstract.
- **Value**: The AI Interpretation Layer (Layer 4) explains the "Why" behind the math.

3. THE WEALTH ADVISOR
==================================================
- **Goal**: Communicate risk and growth to clients.
- **Pain**: Clients' eyes glaze over when looking at charts.
- **Value**: High-impact editorial reports that clients actually enjoy reading.

==================================================

- **Pain**: Expensive consulting or messy internal models.
