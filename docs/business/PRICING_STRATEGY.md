==================================================
1. VALUE PROPOSITION
==================================================
"Stop calculating. Start understanding."
We charge for the **Intelligence** and **Clarity** we provide, not just the calculation power.

==================================================
2. PSYCHOLOGICAL POSITIONING
==================================================
- **Premium Anchor**: Positioning alongside premium subscriptions like Financial Times or Bloomberg (but at a lower price point for individuals).
- **Transparency**: No hidden fees or "per simulation" costs for paid tiers.

==================================================
3. EXPANSION REVENUE
==================================================
- **Model Packs**: One-time purchases for specialized institutional models (e.g., Oil & Gas, Crypto Portfolio).
- **Seat-based Pricing**: For institutional teams.

==================================================
4. SOCIAL IMPACT (The "Student" Tier)
==================================================
- Free "Pro" access for verified educational institutions to drive long-term brand loyalty and financial literacy.
