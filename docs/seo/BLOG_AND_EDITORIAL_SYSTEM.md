1. THE "FINANCIAL LITERACY HUB"
==================================================
The blog is positioned as a "Newsroom" for financial intelligence. It is the primary engine for educational engagement.

==================================================
2. CMS CHOICE
==================================================
- **Headless CMS**: Contentful or Sanity.io.
- **Reason**: Allows editorial teams to manage content without touching the codebase while keeping the frontend fast.

==================================================
3. CONTENT TYPES
==================================================
- **Educational Explainer**: Long-form text with embedded simulations.
- **Model Walkthrough**: Step-by-step guides on using specific tools.
- **Analytical Opinion**: AI-assisted commentary on market trends.

==================================================
4. VISUAL STANDARDS
==================================================
- All blog posts must adhere to the `DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md`.
- No generic stock photos; use custom-generated architectural/financial art or simulation snapshots.
