==================================================
1. ENGINE OBJECTIVE
==================================================
Demonstrate the long-term impact of compounding growth and consistent contributions.

==================================================
2. INPUT PARAMETERS
==================================================
- **Principal**: Starting balance.
- **Annual Rate**: Interest percentage.
- **Monthly Contribution**: Added every period.
- **Compounding Frequency**: Monthly, Quarterly, Annually.
- **Horizon**: Years to grow.

==================================================
3. CALCULATION METHOD
==================================================
Standard compound interest formula with recurring deposits.
- Handled iteratively or via annuity formula for speed.

==================================================
4. OUTPUT STRUCTURE
==================================================
- **Timeline**: Period-by-period balance.
- **Interest vs. Principal**: Cumulative breakdown.
- **Final Balance**: The "North Star" metric.

==================================================
5. VISUALIZATION MAPPING
==================================================
- **The Bubble**: Steady, calm expansion. Glow increases as interest outpaces principal contributions.
- **Graphs**: Stacked area chart (Principal vs. Interest).
